# Marketing site decision log

## 17 September 2026 — buyer-first messaging

The marketing site must communicate, within the first few seconds, what Caplist Studio is, what it does, why a professional property-media business should care, how much additional work it creates and what the customer can sell at the end.

The core story is:

**You already did the expensive work → there is more value in the media you captured → Caplist turns that media into additional finished products → you choose what to sell → your business keeps the client and controls the price.**

## 17 September 2026 — homepage concept comparison

Two homepage concepts will be compared before choosing the final presentation:

- Concept A: editorial / text-led
- Concept B: animated product walkthrough

Both use the same buyer-first message and brand system so the comparison tests communication method rather than different positioning.

## 17 September 2026 — Interactive Floorplan

Interactive Floorplan remains excluded from the current marketing site and product catalogue until a working prototype is available to show and share.
