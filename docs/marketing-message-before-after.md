# Caplist Studio marketing message: before → after

This document records the buyer-facing messaging rewrite approved on 17 September 2026. It is intended to make the change in positioning visible and reviewable before deployment.

## Messaging standard now being applied

Every public section should help a first-time professional property-media visitor answer one or more of these questions:

1. What is Caplist Studio?
2. What does it do with media I already capture?
3. Why should I care commercially?
4. How much extra work does it create for me?
5. What can I sell at the end?
6. Do I keep my client, workflow, brand and pricing control?

The central story is now:

**You already did the expensive work → there is more value in the media you captured → Caplist turns that media into additional finished products → you choose what to sell → your business keeps the client and controls the price.**

## Final message hierarchy

1. **Capture once. Sell more.**
2. **Turn completed property shoots into additional finished products your clients can buy.**
3. **No second property visit and no full manual edit from scratch.**
4. **Upload the media you already have; Caplist shows what else is worth creating.**
5. **Caplist handles production while you control what gets offered, packaged and priced.**
6. **Professional quality is a gate, not an afterthought.**
7. **Keep your workflow, your client relationship and your business in front.**

The desired first-time visitor takeaway is:

> “This takes the professional media I already produce, turns it into extra finished products I can sell to the same client, and I do not need another property visit or another editing workload to do it.”
