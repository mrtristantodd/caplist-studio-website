# DP001 Media QA Checklist

## Review basis

The approved existing image set in [SHOT_LIST.md](SHOT_LIST.md) is canonical. Review derivatives against their exact source and the image-led [Property Bible](PROPERTY_BIBLE.md), not the superseded synthetic architectural requirements. Existing source approval is retained; motion approval is a separate decision.

## Source selection and mapping

- [ ] Source is in the approved hero, reserve, wider library or separately identified approved drone inventory.
- [ ] Exact filename/path and source identity are recorded.
- [ ] Boards/collages and the four excluded files are absent.
- [ ] Duplicate copies are not counted as distinct images.
- [ ] Upload assembly uses exactly 27 approved professional photo images, with approved drone media separately identified and counted only as supported.
- [ ] No invented room labels, drone classifications or original-camera claims are inferred from filenames.

## Same property, styling and material palette

- [ ] Same warm brick exterior, dark tiled roof, footprint and openings as the approved sources.
- [ ] Same interior layout, furniture, cabinetry, fixtures, decor and contemporary/classic styling.
- [ ] Same front garden, rear entertaining zone, mature planting and outdoor styling.
- [ ] Same pool and spa / plunge geometry, position and relationship to the house.
- [ ] Same elevated suburban context and plausible partial water/bush outlook.
- [ ] No invented waterfront, resort scale or geography.
- [ ] No imposed Colorbond roof, hypothetical room programme or unsupported synthetic site orientation.

## Believable motion and no generative drift

Review the entire clip, including its first and final frames, against the approved source.

- [ ] Movement is subtle, physically plausible and suitable for premium real-estate presentation.
- [ ] Architecture, layout, furniture and styling remain fixed throughout.
- [ ] No bending walls, changing windows/doors, invented wings/floors or roof morphing.
- [ ] No pool/spa deformation, moving fixtures, shifting furniture or morphing landscaping.
- [ ] No impossible reflections, malformed details, flicker or texture drift.
- [ ] Camera movement does not reveal fabricated spaces or unsupported geometry.
- [ ] No introduced people or new scene elements.
- [ ] Any natural environmental movement remains restrained and does not alter property identity.

Reject a motion clip with structural invention or visible generative drift even when individual frames look attractive. Do not regenerate an approved still merely because a motion model failed to preserve it.

## Consistent grade and photographic standard

- [ ] Approved late-afternoon/golden-hour lighting character is preserved.
- [ ] Neutral whites, accurate brick/roof/material colour and natural timber warmth.
- [ ] Believable foliage, water and sky; no excessive yellow/orange or artificial cyan.
- [ ] Lighting direction remains stable within the clip and credible across matching views.
- [ ] Controlled highlights and window detail; no obvious HDR halos, crushed blacks or artificial lift.
- [ ] Professional composition, plausible perspective and level architectural verticals.
- [ ] Sharp enough for premium web presentation without excessive cropping or enlargement.
- [ ] Premium Australian real-estate finish; no cinematic fantasy or resort-campaign restyling.

## Reel and walkthrough

- [ ] A1 is a finished 16:9 Mixed Media Reel using the approved hero sources and approved drone media.
- [ ] A2 uses the same approved property media for a 9:16 derivative without distorting architecture or inventing a new view.
- [ ] Not every upload image is forced into the edit.
- [ ] The React/CSS homepage walkthrough is distinct from the actual reel it presents.
- [ ] Upload wording is supported: 27 professional photos · Drone media; no unsupported drone count or professional-video input.
- [ ] Higgsfield output contains no generated Caplist UI, logos, text, buttons or progress bars.
- [ ] Silent playback works; any sound control reflects actual audio, and reduced-motion presentation is available at integration review.

## Approval statuses

For newly generated derivatives:

**GENERATED → REVIEW → APPROVED → MASTER → WEB → LIVE**

Only APPROVED assets may enter production. The user's approved existing still set does not need to pass through generation again. Record its existing approval and retain source provenance.

A failed derivative remains in REVIEW with its rejection reason; do not use it as a reference or advance it downstream. MASTER, WEB and LIVE preserve approval rather than replacing it. Source approval does not approve a generated clip, finished edit or website release automatically.
