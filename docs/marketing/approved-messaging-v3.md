1. What Caplist Studio is

Caplist Studio is B2B SaaS for professional real-estate photographers, videographers and property-media businesses.

Its commercial principle is:

Capture once. Create more. Sell more.

Professional property-media businesses have already incurred the expensive part of a property job:

winning the booking
travel
equipment
property access
professional capture
primary editing

Caplist helps the same professional media create additional commercial value after the original delivery.

It takes existing:

professional property photography
professional landscape property video
drone photography and footage
professional/native vertical footage

and turns those assets into additional finished visual products the media business can sell to the same client.

Caplist does not become the agent’s marketing platform.

The media business keeps:

the client relationship
its own brand
its own service
its own packaging
its own retail pricing

The agent receives a broader range of professional visual content from the property-media provider they already know and trust.

2. Core audience

The primary audience is:

Professional real-estate photographers, videographers and established property-media businesses.

It is not primarily:

agents
vendors
consumers
people trying to turn poor phone footage into premium content

The principle remains:

Professional input → professional output.

4. Messaging philosophy

Maintain the customer-as-hero / StoryBrand-influenced narrative and the broader Seth Godin-style reframing.

The central story is:

The property-media business already creates something valuable.
Most of that value currently ends with the first delivery.
Agents increasingly need more visual content across a property campaign.
Caplist helps the media business turn the assets it already captured into a broader product offering.
The photographer sells those products through its existing client relationship.

The strongest category idea is:

You are already producing the raw material for a much larger product offering.

The website should communicate the opportunity before the mechanism.

5. Copy rules

Every customer-visible section should do at least one of these things:

increase desire
make the commercial opportunity more tangible
reduce perceived effort
build trust
explain an unfamiliar product
move the visitor toward the next appropriate action

If it does none of those things, remove it.

Use:

Benefit → practical outcome → mechanism

rather than:

technology → feature → explanation → eventual benefit

Avoid internal/product-team language such as:

orchestration
quality gates
pipeline
reseller mechanics
roadmap
source-availability logic
COGS
wholesale margin
production architecture

Do not publicly lecture visitors about limitations.

The marketing site sells the opportunity.

The app handles project-specific availability.

Documentation handles technical requirements and edge cases.

6. Important capability language

Use confident product language where the capability is part of Caplist.

Prefer:

Caplist works with professional property photography, property video, drone and native vertical footage.

Do not weaken established capabilities unnecessarily with phrases like:

“Caplist can potentially…”

or:

“may be able to…”

13. Showcase quality benchmark

The final showcase is not allowed to look like a generic slideshow.

The product target is:

A clean, premium property + photo-motion + drone/location reel capable of competing with or exceeding strong existing property-media products.

The marketing showcase establishes the intended quality bar for the production pipeline.

Do not reduce the showcase quality merely because the current production pipeline is still catching up.

At the same time, do not make explicit claims that an unverified production pipeline created an output when that has not yet been proven.
