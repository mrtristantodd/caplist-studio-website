# Interactive Floorplan scope decision

Status: **Future product / excluded from current marketing site**

Decision date: 17 September 2026

Interactive Floorplan remains a future Caplist Studio product concept, but it is deliberately excluded from the current public product catalogue, homepage concepts, product examples, pricing examples and launch messaging.

It should not appear publicly until Caplist has a working prototype that can be shown and shared credibly.

Before it returns to marketing scope, the product should have:

1. a working prototype;
2. confirmed source-input requirements;
3. a clear customer workflow;
4. a defined finished deliverable;
5. a credible commercial product that a property-media business can sell.

Until then, the launch product set remains:

- Photo Reel
- Vertical Video Reel
- Property Teaser
- Feature Reel
- Mixed Media Reel

This is a positioning and product-discipline decision, not a cancellation of the Interactive Floorplan idea.
